❓ IPTV FAQ

Answers to the most commonly asked questions about IPTV services, setup, and technical requirements.

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers video content over an internet connection rather than through a coaxial cable or satellite dish. You subscribe to a service, receive login credentials or a playlist URL, and watch via an app on your device. The main differences: IPTV is typically more affordable, works on more devices, and doesn't require physical installation.
Minimum speeds by quality: SD (480p): 5 Mbps. HD (720p): 10 Mbps. Full HD (1080p): 15–25 Mbps. 4K (2160p): 30–50 Mbps. A wired Ethernet connection is strongly recommended over Wi-Fi for stable, buffer-free streaming. If you experience buffering, try moving closer to your router or using a wired connection.
IPTV works on a wide range of devices: Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Hisense, Sony with Android TV), Android TV Boxes, Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick, smartphones and tablets (Android & iOS), Windows and Mac computers, MAG set-top boxes, and media players like Kodi and VLC. Most modern services provide apps for the major platforms.

A VPN is not required by the services we recommend. Some users use a VPN for general privacy reasons. However, be aware that routing your stream through a VPN server can increase latency and reduce stream quality, especially on slower VPN connections. If you choose to use one, select a VPN server with a fast, nearby location.

Buffering is usually caused by: (1) insufficient internet speed — check you meet the requirements above; (2) Wi-Fi interference — switch to Ethernet if possible; (3) server load on the provider's side during peak hours; (4) outdated player app — keep it updated. If buffering persists with a good connection, contact your provider's support.
An M3U playlist is a text file containing links to streaming sources. Your IPTV provider will give you either an M3U URL to paste into a player like VLC or TiviMate, or Xtream Codes credentials (server URL + username + password) for apps that support them. Xtream Codes is more feature-rich and recommended when available.
EPG (Electronic Program Guide) shows you what's currently playing and what's coming up — similar to a TV guide. Most quality IPTV services provide an EPG URL alongside your playlist. Players like TiviMate and IPTV Smarters display this guide when you add the EPG URL in settings.
Consider these factors: (1) Device compatibility — does the service have an app for your TV/box? (2) Number of streams you need; (3) Video quality — do they offer FHD or 4K? (4) Trial availability — always test first; (5) Customer support responsiveness. Our comparison page ranks the top 5 services across all these criteria.
