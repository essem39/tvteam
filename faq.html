❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about choosing a device and setting up IPTV with TV.TEAM.

What is an Android TV Box?+
A small device that plugs into your TV (usually via HDMI) and runs Android TV, letting you install streaming apps like TV.TEAM on any TV — even older ones without smart features.
Do I need an Android TV Box if I have a Smart TV?+
Not necessarily. If your Smart TV runs Android TV / Google TV, you can install TV.TEAM directly. A box is useful for older TVs or if you want better performance.
Which device category should I choose?+
Budget boxes are fine for standard streaming. Choose Premium if you multitask or run heavier apps, and 4K/8K if your TV supports higher resolutions.
How do I set up IPTV after buying a device?+
Connect the device, install TV.TEAM, and sign in with your subscription. See our full IPTV setup guide for details.
Is TV.TEAM the only IPTV service you recommend?+
Yes. We focus on one partner, TV.TEAM, that we personally set up and test on every device we cover, rather than listing many services.
What internet speed do I need?+
A stable connection of at least 15-25 Mbps is recommended for smooth HD/4K streaming; wired Ethernet is more reliable than Wi-Fi.
