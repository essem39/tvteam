📡 IPTV on Your Android TV Box

What IPTV is, why it works well on a TV box, and how to get streaming with TV.TEAM.

1. Choose your device

Any Android TV Box, Smart TV with Android TV / Google TV, or Fire TV device works. See our device categories if you need one.

2. Connect to the internet

Wi-Fi works, but a wired connection gives the most stable stream for HD/4K channels.

3. Install TV.TEAM

Download the TV.TEAM app from their site or the store on your device, and sign in with your subscription.

4. Load your channels

TV.TEAM sets up your channel list and EPG automatically after sign-in — no manual playlist needed.

5. Start watching

Browse live channels, use catch-up/VOD if included in your plan, and enjoy on the big screen.
