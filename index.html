❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about IPTV

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers live and on-demand video content over the internet instead of traditional cable or satellite. You need a subscription, a compatible device, and a stable internet connection (minimum 10 Mbps recommended for HD).
For HD streaming: 10 Mbps minimum. For Full HD: 15–25 Mbps. For 4K content: 30–50 Mbps. A wired (Ethernet) connection is always preferred over Wi-Fi for more stable playback.
Most IPTV services offer multi-stream packages (2–5 simultaneous connections). You can typically use Smart TV, phone, tablet, and PC at the same time depending on your plan.
A VPN is not strictly required by most services but some users prefer it for privacy. Note that a VPN can reduce stream speed, so always test your connection with and without it.
